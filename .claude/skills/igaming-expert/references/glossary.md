# iGaming glossary

Generic definitions of common industry terms. They describe general practice
across operators, not this company's rules. Thresholds, delays, amounts and
accepted documents vary by operator and jurisdiction: always check the internal
procedure for the company's version.

## Account and verification (KYC / AML)
- **KYC (Know Your Customer)**: checks an operator runs to confirm who the player
  is (identity, age, address). Required by licences and anti-money-laundering rules.
- **Verification levels / tiers**: KYC done in stages. A basic level may allow
  playing, higher levels are often required to withdraw or above certain
  activity. What unlocks each level is set by each operator.
- **Proof of identity (POI)**: official ID document (ID card, passport, driving
  licence, depending on what the operator accepts).
- **Proof of address (POA)**: recent document showing the player's name and
  address (utility bill, bank statement…), usually with a maximum age.
- **Selfie / liveness check**: photo or short video of the player, often holding
  their ID, to prove the document belongs to the person using the account and
  that it is a live person, not a photo of a photo.
- **Source of funds (SOF)**: where the money used to gamble comes from (salary,
  savings, sale of an asset…). Requested to rule out money laundering and, often,
  to check affordability. Supported by documents like payslips or bank statements.
- **Source of wealth (SOW)**: how the player built their overall wealth. Broader
  than SOF, usually asked for high-value players.
- **EDD (Enhanced Due Diligence)**: deeper checks triggered by higher risk or
  higher activity, often including SOF/SOW.
- **AML (Anti-Money Laundering)**: rules and controls to prevent gambling accounts
  being used to launder money.
- **PEP (Politically Exposed Person)**: someone with a prominent public role (or
  close to one), subject to extra checks under AML rules.
- **Sanctions screening**: checking players against official sanctions lists.
- **Affordability check**: assessment of whether the player's spending is
  sustainable for them. Required in some jurisdictions.
- **Duplicate / multi-accounting**: one person holding several accounts, usually
  forbidden by the T&C.

## Payments
- **Deposit**: money a player adds to their account.
- **Withdrawal / cash out (payment sense)**: money a player takes out of their
  account. Distinct from sportsbook "cash out".
- **Pending withdrawal**: withdrawal requested but not yet processed, often while
  checks (KYC, risk, bonus) are done.
- **Reverse withdrawal**: player cancelling a pending withdrawal to put the money
  back on the balance. Some operators allow it, others disable it for
  responsible gambling reasons.
- **Closed loop / same-method rule**: withdrawals sent back to the method used to
  deposit, as far as possible, to limit fraud and laundering.
- **Payment method verification**: proof that the player owns the card, e-wallet
  or bank account used (e.g. masked card photo, screenshot of the wallet).
- **Processing time**: time the operator takes to approve a withdrawal. Different
  from the time the bank or payment provider then takes to credit it.
- **Chargeback**: the player's bank reverses a card payment at the player's
  request, pulling money back from the operator. Can be legitimate (fraud,
  unauthorised use) or abusive ("friendly fraud"). Usually leads to the account
  being restricted while it's investigated.
- **Refund**: the operator voluntarily returns a payment. Not the same as a
  chargeback, which goes through the bank.
- **PSP (Payment Service Provider)**: company processing payments for the operator.

## Bonuses and promotions
- **Bonus funds**: promotional money that can't be withdrawn until its conditions
  are met.
- **Wagering requirement (playthrough, rollover)**: how many times a bonus (or
  bonus + deposit) must be bet before winnings from it can be withdrawn. Example:
  €10 bonus with x30 wagering means €300 of bets.
- **Game weighting / contribution**: share of each bet that counts toward
  wagering, depending on the game (e.g. slots count fully, table games less or not
  at all).
- **Max bet rule**: maximum stake allowed per bet while a bonus is active.
- **Max win / max cashout**: cap on what can be withdrawn from bonus winnings.
- **Bonus expiry**: time limit to use the bonus or complete wagering.
- **Free spins**: free rounds on a slot; winnings are often credited as bonus
  funds with their own wagering.
- **Free bet**: sportsbook stake offered by the operator; usually only the
  winnings are paid, not the stake.
- **No-deposit bonus**: bonus given without a deposit.
- **Sticky / non-sticky bonus**: sticky bonus can never be withdrawn itself;
  non-sticky ("parachute") lets the player play with real money first, bonus used
  only once real money is gone.
- **Bonus abuse**: using promotions against their intent (multi-accounting,
  low-risk betting patterns, breaking max bet…). Usually leads to bonus removal
  under the T&C.
- **T&C / bonus terms**: the contractual rules of the site and of each promotion.

## Responsible gambling
- **Responsible gambling (RG) / safer gambling**: tools and duties that protect
  players from gambling harm.
- **Deposit limit**: cap on deposits over a period (day, week, month). Lowering
  it usually applies immediately; raising it usually comes with a waiting period.
- **Loss / wager / session limits**: caps on losses, stakes or time spent.
- **Reality check**: pop-up reminding the player how long they've been playing.
- **Time-out / cooling-off**: short, voluntary break (hours to weeks, depending on
  the operator). The account is blocked for that period, then reopens.
- **Self-exclusion**: longer, voluntary block (often months to years, sometimes
  permanent) that usually can't be reversed before the end of the period. Much
  stronger than cooling-off.
- **National self-exclusion register**: scheme run by a regulator in some
  countries that excludes the player from all licensed operators at once.
- **Markers of harm / RG flags**: signs of problem gambling (chasing losses,
  rising deposits, distress, borrowing to play) that support must report
  following the RG process.

## Sportsbook
- **Single / accumulator (acca, combo, parlay)**: one selection vs several
  selections combined in one bet, all of which must win.
- **Odds (decimal, fractional)**: price of a selection; decimal odds × stake =
  total return.
- **Void bet**: bet cancelled and treated as if never placed; the stake is
  returned. Common reasons: event cancelled or postponed beyond the rules,
  obvious price error (palpable error), rule breach. In an accumulator, a void
  leg is usually removed and the rest of the bet stands with recalculated odds.
- **Palpable / obvious error**: clearly wrong odds or market published by
  mistake; the rules usually allow the operator to void or resettle the bet.
- **Cash out**: offer to settle a bet before the event ends, for an amount based
  on the current situation. Availability and amount are set by the operator and
  can change or be suspended at any time.
- **Partial cash out**: cashing out only part of the stake.
- **Settlement / resettlement**: marking a bet as won or lost; resettlement is a
  correction after a result change or error.
- **Market suspension**: temporary closure of a market (e.g. during a goal
  review), during which bets and cash out are unavailable.
- **Stake factor / limits**: per-player maximum stake the trading team can apply.
- **Push**: result where the bet neither wins nor loses (e.g. Asian handicap),
  stake returned.

## Casino
- **RTP (Return to Player)**: theoretical percentage of stakes a game pays back
  over a very large number of rounds. Not a guarantee for a single session.
- **Volatility**: how often and how big a game's wins are.
- **RNG (Random Number Generator)**: system producing random game outcomes,
  audited by testing labs.
- **Game provider**: studio that develops the game; some issues (stuck round,
  malfunction) need a check with the provider.
- **Malfunction voids all pays and plays**: common rule that a game fault cancels
  the affected rounds.

## Fraud and risk
- **Risk team**: team reviewing suspicious activity (fraud, abuse, linked accounts).
- **Linked accounts**: accounts connected by shared data (device, IP, payment,
  address). Support should never disclose such suspicions to the player.
- **Account restriction / suspension**: partial or full block during checks.
- **Account closure**: permanent closure, by the player or the operator.
