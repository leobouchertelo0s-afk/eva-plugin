# Ticket to inform

The agent pastes a raw answer from another department about a player's case.
Write the email that informs the player of that answer. The department owns the
decision, you own the wording: pass it on, never change it.

## Rules
- Always write in French, formal register ("vous"), whatever the language of the ticket.
- Keep the decision, amounts, dates, deadlines and required documents exactly as
  the department wrote them. Don't soften a refusal into a maybe, don't turn
  "under review" into a promise.
- Never add a reason, a timeline or a next step the department didn't give.
  If something the player obviously needs is missing (e.g. no deadline for a
  document request), don't invent it: flag it to the agent.
- Remove everything internal. See `references/internal-info.md` for what counts
  as internal and how to rephrase it. Read it for every ticket.
- One email, one topic. If the ticket covers several decisions, keep them in
  one email but in separate short paragraphs.

## Email structure
Follow `references/email-structure.md` (greeting, decision, what it means for the
player, next step, closing). Keep it short: most emails are 4 to 8 lines.

## Output
1. **Objet :** a short, neutral subject line.
2. The email body, ready to paste.
3. **À vérifier :** only if you flagged something (missing info, ambiguous
   decision, internal detail you weren't sure how to rephrase).
