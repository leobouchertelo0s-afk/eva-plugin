# What counts as internal information

The player must never see how the company works inside. If a line in the ticket
matches one of these, remove it or rephrase it as shown.

| Internal (never in the email) | What the player sees instead |
|---|---|
| Names of internal teams or people ("Risk team", "Julien from Payments") | "nos équipes", "le service concerné" |
| Internal tools, back-office names, ticket IDs | nothing |
| Risk, fraud or AML scores, flags, suspicions | nothing. Give only the decision and what the player must do |
| "Bonus abuse", "multi-accounting", "fraud" as an accusation | "conformément à nos conditions générales" + the article if the ticket gives it |
| Internal debates, hesitations, "we're not sure" | only the final decision. If there is none, flag it to the agent |
| Internal deadlines (SLA, "escalate if no answer in 48h") | nothing, unless the ticket says to tell the player |
| Other players' data or linked accounts | nothing, ever |
| Provider or payment partner internals ("PSP", processor names, error codes) | "notre prestataire de paiement" or nothing |

When in doubt, leave it out and add it to **À vérifier**.
